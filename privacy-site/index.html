YOUR CYCLE · PRIVACY

Private by default. Yours alone.

Your Cycle does not collect, receive, sell, share, or remotely process personal data.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Data on your device

Period dates, daily logs, symptoms, notes, reminders, language, and lock settings stay in a SQLCipher-encrypted database on your device. The encryption key stays in the device secure keychain.

No accounts or tracking

There is no account, server, advertising, analytics, telemetry, cloud synchronization, or remote notification token registration.

Notifications and exports

Optional reminders are scheduled locally. PDF and CSV exports are plaintext after leaving the app. The app deletes its temporary copy after sharing or at the next launch.

Biometrics

Optional Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, or device-passcode authentication is handled by your operating system. The app does not receive or store biometric data.

Your choices

You can export or delete all local data from Settings. Deletion removes records, settings, scheduled reminders, and temporary exports and cannot be undone.

Age and medical notice

The app is intended for ages 13 and older and stores no birth date. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, fertility prediction, or contraception advice.
