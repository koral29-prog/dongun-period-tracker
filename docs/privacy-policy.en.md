# Your Cycle Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Your Cycle is designed to keep cycle and health information private by default.

## Data we collect

We do not collect, receive, sell, share, or remotely process personal data. The app has no account, server, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or cloud synchronization.

## Data stored on your device

Period dates, daily logs, symptoms, notes, reminder preferences, language, and lock settings are stored in a SQLCipher-encrypted database on your device. Its encryption key is stored in the device secure keychain and is not designed to move to another device.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app asks for notification permission and schedules notifications locally on your device. It does not register for remote push notifications or send a notification token to a server. You can use discreet notification text and disable reminders at any time.

## Exports

PDF and CSV exports are created locally. Exported files are plaintext after they leave the app. The app presents the system share sheet and deletes its temporary copy when sharing ends or the next time the app launches. A copy saved or sent through another app is controlled by that app and by you.

## App lock

Optional Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, or device-passcode authentication is handled by the operating system. Your Cycle does not receive or store biometric information.

## Your choices

You can export or delete your local data in Settings. Deleting all data removes period records, daily logs, settings, scheduled reminders, and temporary exports from the device. This cannot be undone.

## Age and medical notice

Your Cycle is intended for ages 13 and older. It does not store a birth date. The app is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, fertility prediction, or contraception advice. Seek professional care for medical questions or concerning symptoms.
